Equifax partnership sync - Oct 16
Attendees: Jack, Cyrus (HomeLover); Dana (Equifax, Partnerships lead), Priya (Equifax, Product)

Key outcomes
- Equifax wants to offer HomeLover as a perk inside its employer benefits bundle.
- Both sides see a small pilot as the right first step.

Decisions
- The pilot runs with 3 Equifax employer clients, starting in January.
- HomeLover is listed as a featured partner in the Equifax benefits portal.
- Equifax owns the employer relationship; HomeLover does not contact pilot employers directly.
- One co-branded one-pager instead of separate decks.
- Dana and Jack hold a monthly check-in during the pilot.

Economic model
- Revenue share: Equifax keeps 20% of subscription revenue from members it refers.
- Employers pay nothing during the pilot; members pay the standard monthly plan.
- After the pilot, a per-employer platform fee of $500 a month is on the table.

Product ideas

- A shared dashboard showing pilot enrollment.

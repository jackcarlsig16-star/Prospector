## Part B — Insights (NEW)
A panel "Why performance looks like this", directly under the chart. The top 3 also feed the
existing alerts row (extend design-v1's alertRules.js; do NOT build a second rules system).

DECIDED
- Deterministic rules only, in ONE file (e.g. api/sales/insightRules.js) with the thresholds as
  named constants (REVISABLE). No AI-generated text in v1.
- Each insight has: id, severity (bad/warn/info), title, evidence (the actual numbers and the
  window they came from), likely cause (phrased as "likely" or "possible", never certain),
  suggested action, and affected items (sequence, mailbox or week) as clickable filters.
- An insight fires only if its minimum sample size is met; otherwise it is suppressed and listed
  under "Not enough data to judge: …". Never conclude from tiny samples.
- Each card has a "Dismiss for 7 days" option, stored in the DB with who and when.

Starter rule set. Use only fields confirmed in Stage 1; any rule its data can't support is
reported and skipped:
  R1 List quality: a sequence or week with bounce > 5% (≥50 sent) → "Likely bad or outdated
     contact data" → verify the list before resuming, and consider pausing the sequence.
  R2 Sender reputation: spam-block > 2% (≥50 sent) → "Likely domain/mailbox reputation or no
     warmup" → enable warmup, check SPF/DKIM/DMARC, lower daily volume.
  R3 Hidden spam placement: bounce and spam-block falling while the open rate also drops ≥50%
     vs the prior 2 full weeks (≥200 sent) → "Emails may be landing in spam even though
     'delivered'" → seed or inbox-placement test, warmup, and content check.
  R4 Engagement drought: 0 replies across ≥200 sent in the last 14 days → "Messaging or
     targeting isn't landing" → review copy and personas (point to the best-performing sequence
     as a contrast).
  R5 Sending stopped or dropped: volume down ≥40% week over week (full weeks), OR a mailbox
     with a connection error → "Sends may have stalled" → reconnect the mailbox, check sequence
     status.
  R6 Capacity: a mailbox averaging more than its daily cap (constant DAILY_CAP_PER_MAILBOX = 50,
     REVISABLE) over 7 days → "Mailbox at capacity; deliverability risk" → add sending
     mailboxes (ideally on a secondary domain).
  R7 Step fatigue: within a sequence, the open rate of step N+1 < 50% of step N (≥30 delivered
     per step) → "Drop-off after step N" → rework that step or shorten the cadence. Needs
     per-step data; skip it if Stage 1 says that data isn't available.
  R8 Outlier sequence: a sequence whose reply rate is ≤ ½ the blended average while having
     ≥100 delivered → "Underperforming vs your other sequences" → compare its copy and persona
     to the best one.
  R9 Bot-inflated opens: in sales_email_activity, ≥40% of opens flagged as likely bot (fast
     open, or a scanner user agent) → "Open rate is inflated; trust replies/clicks" → label the
     open-rate KPI accordingly.
  R10 Good news (info): a metric moved into the green vs the prior full week → say so plainly.

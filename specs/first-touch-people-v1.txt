## Problem
1. The first-touched metric counts a partner once in the week of its earliest contact. There is no people-level
   count anywhere, so 2 people emailed at BenefitHub = 1.
2. Set goal has no unit. A number typed as "100 people" is read as "100 partners".
3. The card's label, number, ring, and sparkline all speak partners; nothing shows the people side even as a
   sub-line.

## Design decision
DECIDED
- The first-touched goal gets a unit: people | partners. Card label follows it: "People first-touched" or
  "Partners first-touched". Primary number + ring + sparkline in the chosen unit; the other unit on the
  sub-line: "12 of 100 people · 5 partners · week 1 · Oct 5". Set goal dialog gets the toggle.
- Migrate Jack's existing HomeLover first-touched goal (100) to unit = people. Default for new goals = people.
- A person = a partner_contacts row (by id), or for a touch naming someone not in partner_contacts, (goal_id,
  normalized name). Reuse the People merge from partner-360 Stage 1 (src/constants/partnerPeople.js) so the
  card and the drop-down agree on who is one person.
- First touch for a person = the earliest of: a touch event whose contact_names includes them (event date);
  partner_contacts.sequence_added_at (Apollo); an Apollo auto-touch event naming them. Counted ONCE, in the week
  of that earliest date; backdated touches land in the right week (same rule as partners). Outlook (microsoft
  Stage 3) feeds the same path later — build the function once with a sources list.
- No touch = not touched: Sheet-only names (known_contacts) and Apollo rows with no sequence and no touch do
  not count.
- Drill-down: clicking the number lists the week's people — name · partner · first touch date · source
  (Logged / Apollo) — same pattern as the existing drill lists.
- Weekly report / scorecard row for first-touched mirrors the unit.
PROPOSED / REVISABLE
- Label wording "People first-touched". Sparkline history recomputed for past weeks from events (cheap; events
  are the source of truth) rather than stored snapshots — flag back if the scorecard stores weekly snapshots and
  this would disagree with them.

## Out of scope
- "People in sequence" card (Apollo prospects, different population) — unchanged. Companies/cadence metrics.
- Outlook as a source (microsoft-connect-v1 Stage 3 adds it through the same function).
- Any change to how partners first-touched is computed.

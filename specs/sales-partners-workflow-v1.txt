## Problem (real, grounded)
1. Buttons have no key. partners/PartnerCard.js:23-27 renders the stage buttons icon-only (✉ ↩ 📅 🧪 ✅);
   the label exists only as a hover title (line 80). On a phone there is no hover, so nothing explains them.
2. Clicking "just stays like that". PartnerCard.js:80 sets disabled={status === b.to}: the clicked button
   greys out and nothing says what changed, what stage the partner is now in, or what comes next.
3. The columns confuse. PartnersView.js:186-188 lays partners out as P1 / P2 / P3 / Unprioritized columns
   (or 10 pipeline-status columns). Category, the main way Jack thinks about partners (14 categories in
   HomeLover_Partner_Owners.xlsx), is only a filter chip, and stage progress isn't visible at a glance.
4. No manual order. Within a column, order is fixed; Jack can't move a partner up or down.
5. No visual partner type. All cards look the same regardless of category.

## Design decision

DECIDED — new default view: "Workflow" (replaces Priority columns)
- Toggle becomes [Workflow | Board]. Workflow is the default. Board = the existing pipeline-status
  columns, unchanged. The P1/P2/P3 column layout is removed (priority lives on as a chip + sort key).
  Remembered per viewer in localStorage as today (try/catch).
- Top of view: one overall progress bar — a horizontal stacked bar of all partners by stage
  (Not started → Live), each segment clickable to filter (goals-surface Stage 1 ring rule: "Showing: X ×"
  chip clears). Under it, the KEY (see below).
- Below: one group per category, in category-number order (1. Current Partners … 14. Co-opetition).
  Group header: type color marker · category name · "3 of 7 touched" · a thin group progress bar
  (share of partners past Not started/Researching) · owner split dots.
- Each group shows its TOP 5 rows, then "Show 4 more ▾". Top = manual rank first, then priority
  (P1 > P2 > P3 > none), then hot, then tier (1 > 2 > 3 > 4), then name. Hot and P1 partners therefore
  sit at the top unless Jack has dragged something above them.
- A "Top priorities" strip above the groups: the 5 highest-ranked P1/hot partners across all
  categories, same row design, so the most important work is the first thing on screen.

DECIDED — partner row (the workflow)
- Left edge: 4px color marker for partner TYPE (see families below) + rank controls (drag handle on
  desktop; ▲ ▼ buttons always, so it works on phone and keyboard).
- Name · owner color dot (Jack #6F8CF0, Cyrus #C97626) · P chip · Tier chip · 🔥 if hot.
- STAGE PROGRESS BAR: 8 labeled steps — Not started · Researching · Drafted · Sent · Replied · Meeting ·
  Pilot · Live (in_sequence counts as Sent; Paused shows the bar greyed with a "Paused" pill). Filled
  steps in the type color; current step labeled; on phone only the current step's label shows.
- ONE primary text button: "Next: <next step>" (e.g. "Next: Mark sent", "Next: Meeting booked").
  Clicking it advances one stage through applyPartnerSignal (same signals, same events, same FIX-2
  version check). Nothing is icon-only anymore.
- "⋯ More" menu with text labels: Jump to stage… (any stage, incl. back a step) · Assign · Hot on/off ·
  Snooze 7 days · Deprioritize · Add note.
- AFTER ANY CLICK (fixes problem 2): the bar animates to the new step; the row shows "Moved to
  Meeting · today" for the rest of the session; a toast says "Upside → Meeting booked · Next: send pilot
  proposal · Undo" (5s, as today). If the server refuses (FIX-2 race), the toast says
  "Changed by someone else — refreshed" and the row reloads.
- Days since touch stays on the row (red ≥7, as today).

DECIDED — drop-down for research, intel and notes
- Click the row (or ▾) to expand inline: Angle · Next step · Partner role · Known contacts ·
  Target titles · Sequence to use + "Open in Apollo" · Flags/Do-not-say (amber) · Notes ·
  History (event timeline, newest first, with who and when; "from Outlook" labels later).
- One expanded row at a time per group; Escape closes.

DECIDED — the KEY (fixes problem 1)
- A compact, always-visible legend under the overall bar: the 8 stages with their order, the type
  colors with family names, and "Next = moves one step · ⋯ = everything else". Collapsible to one line;
  collapsed state remembered per viewer.

PROPOSED / REVISABLE — type color families (14 categories → 6 colors; 14 colors is unreadable)
  Current partners ............ cat 1
  Platforms & marketplaces .... cats 2, 7, 8
  Brokers, consultants & PEOs . cats 3, 4, 6
  Renters & financial wellness  cats 5, 9
  Influence & public sector ... cats 10, 12, 13
  Professional & co-opetition . cats 11, 14
  Map lives in one constant in code, derived from the category number prefix. Pick 6 accessible colors
  from the Goals tokens; do NOT reuse the member blue/orange. Flag back if a mapping looks wrong.
PROPOSED / REVISABLE — top 5 per group; group order fixed by category number (no group reordering).

DECIDED — data
- One new column: sales_goals.sort_rank numeric (null = unranked, sorts after ranked). Moving a row
  writes a new rank between its neighbours (fractional ranking, so one row update per move). Rank is
  per workspace, shared by Jack and Cyrus (it's the team's order).
- Rank changes go through a new API route (Member+; Viewer refused; partner must belong to the
  caller's workspace). No event row for reorders (they're not partner activity).

## Out of scope (and why)
- Scorecard math, partner events, Huddle Partners group, weekly report chips — unchanged; they read
  the same data.
- Account cards (standing rule: don't touch).
- Group (category) reordering and per-person private order — wait until Jack has used shared order.
- Outlook auto-signals — microsoft-connect-v1.
- Editing research/intel fields inline in the drop-down — read-only display in this spec; editing stays
  where it is today. Follow-on if Jack wants it: sales-partners-inline-edit-v1.

## Standing rules that apply here (inlined)
- All writes go through the API. Since FIX-1 the browser cannot write sales_goals; do not add any
  direct Supabase write from the client.
- Partner stage changes ONLY through applyPartnerSignal (the Outlook hook depends on it). Owner
  changes ONLY through Assign (FIX-7).
- Keep the FIX-2 concurrency check on every signal; reorders may be last-write-wins.
- Viewer (Seif) sees everything, clicks nothing: no Next button, no ⋯, no drag/▲▼.
- 0 Apollo writes (read-only rule). inbox/*.xlsx is never committed.
- Tests that write data restore and report "restored: yes". Run test suites one at a time.

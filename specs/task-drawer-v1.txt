SPEC — task-drawer-v1

## Status
Source: Jack in chat, 2026-10-06: "there could be a sidebar — an easy pull-out menu for Cyrus and I, so
we can navigate to any page and then add something to a task or cross off a task or even assign to goals."
Builds on what exists: Goals to-dos (sales_week_goals kind 'todo' with steps, owner, contacts, carry-over,
flags from the Huddle). This is a second, always-available VIEW of those same to-dos — NOT a new task
system. It is also the home for two follow-ons: "Paste call notes" (call-notes-to-tasks-v1 REVISION 1)
and "Log outreach" (sales-quick-add-research-v1).
QUEUED: after goals-surface-v1 Stage 5, BEFORE sales-quick-add-research-v1 (so Log outreach can land in
the drawer). call-notes-to-tasks REVISION 1 builds right after this.

## Problem (real, grounded)
1. Adding, completing or reassigning a to-do requires going to Goals → This week; from Huddle, Overview,
   Companies or any other page there's no way to capture a task without leaving the page.
2. Flags created in the Huddle land as Goals to-dos and a "Flagged for you" lane/badge in the Huddle
   only — not visible from other pages.
3. A to-do can't be tied to the goal it serves (a weekly commitment or a metric goal), so the goal
   doesn't show the work behind it.

## Design decision

DECIDED — the drawer
- A "Tasks" button in the top bar on every workspace page where Goals & Sales is enabled (businesses.features
  goals_sales), with a badge = my open tasks due this week + flagged-for-me. Also a slim handle on the right
  edge (desktop). Opens a right-side drawer (~380px) over the page without navigating away; Escape or ×
  closes; open/closed remembered per viewer (try/catch localStorage). Phone: full-height sheet from the ☰
  header.
- Top: quick-add line — type and press Enter. Defaults: owner = me, week = this week. Inline chips after
  typing: Owner (any workspace member or Unassigned) · Due date · Link (see below). Steps can be added after.
- Filter pills: Me · Cyrus · Team · Unassigned (member list from the workspace, not hard-coded names;
  member colors as on Goals).
- List groups, in order: Flagged for me · Overdue · This week · From calls · Done this week (collapsed).
  Each row: checkbox (complete), title, owner dot, due, link chip, step count "2/4". Click a row to expand:
  steps (check off individually), note, contacts, "Open in Goals" link.
- Check off = the same complete action Goals uses (flag completion, carry-over and report counts behave
  exactly as today). Toast with Undo 5s.
- Reassign from the row's owner chip. Delete limited as FIX-5 (creator within 2 min, or Owner/Admin);
  others get Drop.
- Viewer sees the drawer read-only (no add, check, assign).

DECIDED — "assign to goals" (Link)
- A to-do can link to ONE of: a weekly commitment (sales_week_goals kind 'commitment'), a metric goal
  (sales_metric_targets key, e.g. partners_first_touched), a partner, or a company. Typeahead over all
  four, grouped.
- In Goals, a linked commitment shows "3 tasks · 1 done" under it, expandable; a metric goal card (hero,
  goals-surface Stage 3) shows "2 open tasks" linking to the drawer filtered to it. Partner/company links
  show on the partner row drop-down / company row.
- A linked to-do does NOT change metric math — goals still count real events.

PROPOSED / REVISABLE
- Drawer width 380px; badge counts due-this-week + flagged only (not all open).
- No keyboard shortcut in v1 (avoid clashing with browser/OS shortcuts); add later if wanted.

## Out of scope
- Paste call notes → tasks: call-notes-to-tasks-v1 REVISION 1 (adds a "Paste call notes" button in the
  drawer header). Reserve the header slot.
- Log outreach: sales-quick-add-research-v1 — when it builds, its button ALSO appears in the drawer header.
- Notifications/email reminders, recurring tasks, tasks for non-sales workspaces.
- Owner identity unification (Huddle jack/cyrus enum vs member ids, audit M5) — separate spec; the
  drawer uses member ids like Goals.

- Flag rules from FIX-3/4/5 (undo safety, one open flag per prospect, restricted delete) unchanged.
- Workspace-bound ids on every route; Viewer refused on writes.
- Tests that write data restore and report "restored: yes". Run test suites one at a time.

## Problem
1. The only way to record partner activity is Next / Jump to stage, which stamps TODAY. Past outreach lands
   in the wrong week, so "partners first-touched" per week is wrong.
2. There's no record of WHO at the partner was contacted or HOW (email, call, LinkedIn, meeting, event).
3. Catching up on many partners means one click-flow per partner.

## Design decision

DECIDED — "＋ Log touch" (one form, used everywhere)
- Where: a "Log touch" button on every Partners Workflow row (and in its ⋯ menu), in the partner drop-down
  above History, and a "＋ Log touches" button in the Partners header for bulk. Member+; Viewer sees none.
- Fields: Date (default today; date picker; no future dates) · Type [Email · Call · LinkedIn · Meeting ·
  Event · Other] · Who (one or more contact names, free text with suggestions from the partner's
  known_contacts and earlier touches) · Note (optional) · Move stage to (optional; default "auto").
- Auto stage rule (never moves backwards): an outbound touch (Email/Call/LinkedIn) on a partner at Not
  started / Researching / Drafted → Sent (1st email sent), dated on the touch date. Meeting → Meeting stage
  if behind it. Event/Other → no stage change. An explicit "Move stage to" wins, still never backwards.
- Every touch writes one sales_partner_events row via applyPartnerSignal with the touch date as the event
  time (it already accepts a date), so history, undo, last_touch_at, days-since-touch, scorecard
  "partners first-touched" and the goal card all count it in the right week.
- Undo toast 5s, same as other partner actions (FIX-2 stale-stage guard still applies to stage moves).

DECIDED — bulk catch-up
- "＋ Log touches" opens a table: pick partners (search + checkboxes, or paste names one per line —
  matched to partners, unmatched shown in red to fix), then per row: date · type · who · note · stage.
  "Apply to all" fills a column. Review screen shows current stage → new stage per partner and which
  week each first-touch will count in. Nothing saves until "Log N touches".

DECIDED — contacts on the partner
- The partner drop-down gets a "People contacted" line: distinct names from touches, with last date and
  type ("Matt Buthman · Email · Oct 2"), and a count on the Workflow row ("👤 3").
- Names only (as typed). No emails/phones stored from this form.

PROPOSED / REVISABLE
- Which types count as a "first touch" for the goal: Email, Call, LinkedIn, Meeting (not Event/Other).

## Out of scope
- Outlook auto-touches (microsoft-connect-v1 Stage 3 will call the same path and label "from Outlook").
- Touches for non-partner companies (sales-quick-add-research-v1 "Log outreach").
- Editing or deleting a past touch beyond Undo (follow-on if needed; Owner/Admin can drop via history).
